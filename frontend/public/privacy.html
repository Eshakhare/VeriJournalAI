VeriJournal AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo solely for authentication purposes. We do not collect or store payment information.

How we use your data

Your information is used exclusively to authenticate you and to store your personal news integrity journal entries, which are private to your account.

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Journal entries are stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under your unique user ID and are accessible only to you.

Data retention

You may export or delete your data at any time using the in-app export feature. Upon account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Cloud infrastructure. Access is controlled by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules.

Contact

For any privacy questions, please open an issue on our project repository or contact us via the app.
